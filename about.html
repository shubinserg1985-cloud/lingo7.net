Our mission

To make learning a language feel like what it should feel like: reading a good book.

The problem

The world has changed. The way we learn languages has not.

We live in a remarkable time. Machines understand speech, translate hundreds of books in seconds and write in any language you name.

And yet, to learn a language, we still memorise word lists, work through interchangeable exercises and give up after a fortnight. Not because we are lazy. Because it is boring.

The idea

We put interest at the centre

What if you could learn a language through books you actually want to read? Through summaries of the bestsellers everyone is talking about? Through short pieces on space, psychology, history?

That is how Lingo7 works. You read and listen to what interests you, and the language grows on its own. The translation alongside takes the fear out. Spaced repetition makes the words stay. All of it in one closed loop.

We built Lingo7 to prove a point: a language can be learned with pleasure — without cramming, and without the feeling of homework.

The team

People who are learning languages themselves

We are a small team that knows the problem from the inside. Every one of us has felt how hard motivation is to keep when the process itself gives you nothing.

So we are building the product we use ourselves: 49 languages, material for every level, and steady improvement — because we think everyone deserves good tools for this.
