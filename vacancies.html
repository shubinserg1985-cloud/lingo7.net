Lingo7 is changing how the world learns languages. We are looking for representatives in cities everywhere — people who get into a new method early and earn from bringing it to their city.

I want to be a representative

Why this is an opening, not a side gig

There are millions of language graduates. Most will go on to teach from the same textbooks they were taught from. But the way languages are learned is changing right now: reading what you actually find interesting instead of cramming; large amounts of living language instead of “fill in the article” drills.

You can enter the profession as the ten-thousandth teacher doing the old thing — or you can come in early on this wave, alongside the project creating it. A Lingo7 representative is the person who opens their own city.

What Lingo7 is

Lingo7 turns any book into a personal language course: sentence-by-sentence parallel translation, audio, and a dictionary that reminds you to review words at the right moment. Any pair of languages. You read Harry Potter, a thriller, or a book from your own field — and the language arrives through interest rather than willpower.

What a representative does

You promote Lingo7 in your city, by whatever means you can invent: talking to people, language schools, businesses whose staff need a language, student communities, local chats and social media.

You get a personal promo code. Everyone who subscribes with it is tied to you permanently — and you get your share of every payment they make, renewals included.

Honest arithmetic

Let us count on a working model, not on handing out cards to everyone (models are covered below). Say you have 40 real conversations a day with people who need the language for work, and one in fifteen subscribes — that is 2–3 people a day, 50–60 a month.

A subscription is around $10 a month (in some countries the local price is lower), and your share is 50%. By the end of the first month that is roughly $250–300. And those people keep paying while they use the app: you start the next month with an accumulated base, not from zero. After a few months of this it is $1,000 and up. And one agreement with a hotel manager for a team of 20–30 does the same in a single conversation.

This is an estimate to show the order of magnitude, not a promise: the result depends on the model, the city, and you.

Why this is not a scam

We take nothing from you — no fees, no deposits, no payment for “training”. We earn only when you earn: the user pays for a subscription and half of it is yours.

Your users are tied to your promo code in the system and the accounting is transparent. And the founder walks around Miami with business cards himself — this page was written by someone doing exactly what it offers you.

Earnings come from the model, not from luck

The product is the same, yet two representatives in one city can end up ten times apart. The difference is not luck and not whether the topic is “hot”. The difference is the model: where you are, who you talk to, and what you offer.

Two representatives, one city

The first stands on the beach handing cards to everyone. Three hundred contacts a day, two or three subscriptions per thousand — and a month later he decides it “does not work”.

The second goes to hotels and restaurants, where the language is a condition for the staff’s careers. He shows the app, but negotiates with the manager rather than the waiter: one meeting, twenty or thirty subscriptions for the whole shift, and the hotel pays, not the employee. Same city, same hours, different model — different money.

If it is not working for you, it almost always means one thing: wrong model. That is fixable, and we work through such cases together.

Models people start with

  • Hotels, restaurants, tourism — staff need the language for their careers; the best way in is through the manager.
  • Businesses whose staff deal with foreigners: taxis, salons, clinics, retail.
  • Language schools in your city: introductions, promo codes for students, partnership.
  • Universities and student communities.
  • Local chats and social media: expats, recent arrivals, people preparing to move.

The team

We do not keep the models to ourselves. Representatives share a chat and hold regular calls: who tried what, what the numbers were, what worked. A tactic that lands in one city is available to everyone else the next day.

That is why we look for people with an entrepreneurial streak — the ones who see not “hand out cards”, but “where in my city are the people who need the language for money, and who is willing to pay for them”.

Who this is not for

Let us say it plainly. This is not for you if you:

  • want a fixed salary for hours served;
  • are not ready to talk to strangers;
  • expect results within a week and without effort;
  • want ready instructions for every step and will not try things of your own.

Who we are looking for

Students of linguistics and language programmes — or people seriously in love with languages. Any city in the world. What matters is an entrepreneurial streak: the will to look, try, count and grow in the language field rather than wait for instructions.
