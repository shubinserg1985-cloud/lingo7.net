Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This policy explains what Lingo7 does with information about you. Lingo7 is operated by SEYU LLC, a Florida limited liability company. It covers the website at lingo7.net and the Lingo7 mobile application (together, the “Service”). Questions go to support@lingo7.net.

1. What we collect, and why

  • Account details — your email address, your sign-in credentials, and a display name if you choose one. We need these to create your account, let you sign in and recover access.
  • Learning data — the languages you have chosen, your progress, saved words and their review schedule, reading positions, and the texts you upload. This is what the Service is for: without it there is nothing to show you and nothing to carry between your devices.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active subscription and when it renews, as reported to us by the App Store or Google Play. Payment itself happens in the store. We never receive your card number.
  • Device and technical information — such as device type, operating-system and app version, and app language, together with diagnostic information about faults. We use it to keep the Service working and to know which platforms to support.
  • What you write to us — the content of your support correspondence, so that we can answer it.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location. We do not read your contacts, photos or calendar. We do not use advertising identifiers, we run no advertising trackers, and we do not record your screen or your session.

3. Cookies and the website

The website sets no advertising cookies and runs no analytics that identify you or record what you do on the page. It stores in your browser only what it needs in order to work.

Some elements of our pages, such as web fonts, are delivered by third-party networks. Because your browser fetches them directly, those providers receive the standard information that comes with any web request, including your IP address. We do not send them anything about you beyond that.

4. Automatic translation, audio and the AI discussion

To produce a parallel translation, to read a text aloud and to discuss a chapter with you, the relevant passage is sent to the translation, speech and language-model providers we work with, which process it on our behalf in order to return the result. Please do not put information into the Service that you would not want processed this way.

5. Who else sees your data

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • service providers that help us operate the Service, such as hosting, message delivery and the processing described in section 4, acting on our behalf;
  • the app stores, to the extent needed to operate a subscription bought there;
  • authorities, where the law requires it, and only to the extent it requires;
  • a buyer or successor, if the business is transferred.

6. Where your data is held

We are based in the United States, and your data may be stored and processed there and in other countries where we or our service providers operate. By using the Service you understand that your data may be transferred outside the country you live in.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and afterwards only for as long as we need it for legitimate business purposes or to meet a legal obligation. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your data from the active Service; copies held in routine backups are cleared as those backups are rotated.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect your data against loss and unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Where the law requires us to notify you of a security incident, we will.

9. Your choices

You can see and change your account details and learning data in the app. You may also ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account — write to support@lingo7.net from the address on your account, and we will respond within a reasonable time. Deleting your account is free and needs no reason.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, write to support@lingo7.net and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change materially affects you, we will give notice in the app or by email before it takes effect. The current version always lives at lingo7.net/privacy.html, with the date of the last change at the top.
